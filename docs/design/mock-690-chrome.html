02Nav spacing — shipped vs proposed, at real widths

Eleven items need ~1050px on one row; the left region is ~756px at 1440 and ~1370px at 2560, so the wrap at 1440 is structural. What changes is the geometry of the wrap and of the gaps. Shipped: every item carries two transparent reserved brackets (.pnav-bracket, 3px pad each) and a 14px column gap, so the divider sits in a ~51px hole beside ~36px item gaps, and row 2 indents by a bracket. Three condensed variants below share one mechanism — gap 0, one side-padding knob, no reserved space — and differ in how the row reads as tabs. Click any item to move the active state; nothing shifts in any of them. Ruling on the first mock: "could we condence the dead space more.. make it look more like tabs-esk", which is why the 20px proposal was withdrawn for these.

SIDE PADDING (the one knob)

N1 · TIGHT PATH · the PowerShell-path idiom kept; brackets zero-width on the active item; no separators

Closest to what ships, minus every pixel of reserved space: text-to-text is 2×pad (12px at the ruled 6px), the group divider adds 11px, wrapped rows align. Reads as a path, not as tabs. Not chosen.

N2 · TAB STRIP · a hairline between every tab, active tab filled + amber underline, bracketlessRULED · Corey 2026-09-03 · "2. 6px"

The rule between cells is what makes 13px (6 + the 1px rule + 6) read as "adjacent tabs" rather than "dead space": every gap has a visible edge in it. Across the group divider it is 21px (6 + 4 + 1 + 4 + 6). The active state is the filled cell + the 2px amber underline the app already uses; the brackets go, because a filled cell is the same statement made denser. The group divider is the stronger --border-strong rule. Wrapped rows keep their hairlines, so the second row still reads as tabs.

N3 · BOXED TABS · bordered cells on a baseline rule, the active one opens onto the content, 2px amber top edge

The most literally tab-shaped. Its cost is the wrap: on the 1440 two-row layout the first row's boxes sit over the second row rather than over content, so the "opens onto the content" cue only means something at 2560. Look at it at 756 before choosing it.
