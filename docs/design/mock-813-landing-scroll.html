what's real in this mock, and what changed in v7

v7 is the lobby, not the terminal. The terminal's chrome (the rail, BREAKING, the tapes, the mono caps, the middle dots) stays behind the door; it appears here only inside the live board, which is the product as it is. The page is one family, Plex Sans, at five sizes; amber is spent on the primary button and the funnel's last row and nowhere else; cards exist only for the two live objects (the funnel, the board). Every colour is a token from app/globals.css; nothing is new.

Every row and number is prod's: the funnel (Introduced 112, In committee 15,997, On the floor 473, Passed one chamber 568, On the President's desk 3, Became law 127; 17,280 in all), the week of Nov 2's sentences and the hearings count from / at 2:14 PM MST; the board's rows, the stats and the foot from /welcome at 1:23 PM. The board doesn't cycle here; on the site the panels rotate through all nine datasets, as today. LOGIN stays hidden (ACCOUNT_UI false since HO 796).

Every bill, summarized

Each bill gets a summary written from its text, with its topics. The ceremonial ones, like namings, awareness designations and honoring resolutions, are tagged and set aside from the counts.

Six stages

A bill's stage is read from its latest action and checked against the official list of public laws, so a bill that became law and then received a committee report still counts as a law.
