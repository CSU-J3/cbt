What changed
Nothing is anchored to the far right of a row any more. That was the stretch in every case — the miss strip ended and CHRONIC sat 470px away; a breaking headline ended and its timestamp sat 370px away. Rows are now flex, packed left, and whatever width is left over collects harmlessly at the end instead of opening a gap mid-row.

Absence watch went two-column and shows all six tripped members instead of three plus an overflow link. Two columns of packed rows is what that panel had the width for all along.

The bills column was loud, not dense. Every row carried five bordered or colored elements — boxed bill number, bright title, underlined party-colored sponsor, and two or three outlined topic chips — about 120 competing objects down the column. Now the title is the only bright thing per row; the number is plain amber, topics are plain dim text, chip borders are gone. Color went from decoration to meaning: it only marks stage, so ENACTED reads green and FLOOR amber against dim CMTE, which is the thing you're actually scanning for. Rows group under TODAY / YESTERDAY / EARLIER so the eye has rhythm instead of 26 identical lines.

Left column fills its height — breaking went to 12 rows and the distribution panel shows stage and topic together rather than hiding one behind a tab.
