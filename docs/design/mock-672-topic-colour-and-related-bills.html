PART 1 · what you're ruling. Raw or pulled-down. Either way the treatment is colour on the text only, no border — the bordered branch of TopicChips is already orphaned (both consumers pass plain), and reviving it would undo the HO 609/614/618 border-means-affordance rule and put width back under M3's predicate.

PART 1 · the cost, named up front. V2FeedList.tsx:326 joins every topic into ONE inheriting span today. Colour means one own-style span per topic, so M3 moves off its 5.18 baseline by roughly one to two per row. That is a reported number, not a failure.

PART 1 · the argument, in the repo's own words. C3 as applied is not "one colour per row" — the sponsor kept party colour through HO 609 because V2FeedList.tsx:230 judged it "the only thing that was carrying meaning." Topic colour passes the identical test: it is the same topicColor() value the BY TOPIC bar uses, so the chip and the bar are one fact rendered twice. Record it as a reversal argued on the standing rule, the way HO 632 handled the date-group reversal, not as an override of it.

PART 2 · R1 or R2. R1 is a list. R2 promotes the identical/companion bill because "does this have a twin in the other chamber" is the question the corpus cannot answer today and the rest of the list is context. R2 costs a conditional block that renders only when a companion exists — C4-clean, since nothing is reserved when there is none.

PART 2 · not free of consequence. Every related-bill row is a link inside a panel under a row whose whole surface expands, so each needs stopPropagation and the HO 627 gate grows legs. And the dedup against the HEARING agenda (BillExpandPanel.tsx:335 already filters it against the current bill) is a requirement, not a polish item.

STILL BLOCKED: Part 2 renders nothing real until the related-bill list is ingested. The count may already be free in raw_json ($.relatedBills.count) — probe before planning. The list needs the same per-bill sweep as the cosponsor roster, which is why HO 671 files them as one entry.
