The strip eats ~180px. After the ~130px of constant chrome, that leaves roughly 400px of browser above the fold — the filter bar, the full hearings-plus-committee rail, and about seven member rows before the clip. The overview reads, then you're straight into browsing.

Honest tradeoff at this height: dots go small, so the strip reads as shape and gap, not a dot-by-dot explorer. That's the right job for a header strip. If you want individual dots legible enough to pick one out by eye, it needs ~260px and the browser drops another ~80px. My lean is the 180px overview as shown — the member hub already carries the single-member axis for detail, so this layer only owes you the population shape. Hover still gives the per-member readout and hub link even at this size.
