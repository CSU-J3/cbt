RULED — R2. The identical/companion bill is promoted into its own bordered line above the list, labelled by chamber and relationship, carrying that bill's own introduced date and stage. The remainder falls under an Also related · N label as a flat list with the relationship as a trailing tag, capped with a +N more. R1 (one flat list, no promotion) was the alternative and was not taken.

CONDITIONAL, per C4. No companion → no bordered block and no "Also related" label, just the list. No relations at all → the same NO RELATED BILLS empty state its three neighbours use. Nothing is reserved to report an absence.

DEDUP IS A REQUIREMENT. Section 03. A bill already rendered by the HEARING block's markup agenda must not appear again here. The agenda is already filtered against the current bill at BillExpandPanel.tsx:335; this is the second filter, against the agenda itself.

CLICK TARGETS. Every row here is a link inside a panel under a feed row whose whole surface expands, so each needs stopPropagation and the HO 627 gate grows legs. Counted before the gate runs, not after.

NOT RULED HERE. The cap on the "Also related" list (drawn at 3 + a +N, not measured), and the ordering within it. Both want the real distribution of $.relatedBills.count across the corpus first — which is the probe the ingestion HO opens with.

STILL BLOCKED: related-bill identities are not in the database. The detail endpoint returns a count and a URL, never a list; the list needs a per-bill sweep against /bill/{congress}/{type}/{number}/relatedbills, which is why HO 673 files it in ONE entry with the cosponsor roster — the corpus loop is the expensive half and one pass can fetch both.
