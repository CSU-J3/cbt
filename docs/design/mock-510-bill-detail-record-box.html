Summary

This bill would set the budget and policies for the U.S. military for fiscal year 2027, affecting all Department of Defense (DOD) personnel, programs, and operations. It authorizes funding for equipment like aircraft and ships, sets military personnel levels and compensation, and dictates policy across various areas including defense acquisitions, international relations, cybersecurity, and research. It also directs policy for the Department of Energy's national security programs and authorizes military construction projects.
