Summary

Prohibits federal agencies and recipients of federal construction funds from requiring or prohibiting project labor agreements as a condition of contract award. Directs agencies to award contracts on a competition-neutral basis and preempts conflicting executive-order requirements. Applies to contracts and grants above the simplified acquisition threshold; existing agreements signed before enactment are grandfathered. CBO scores negligible direct spending effect; the fight is over Davis-Bacon adjacency, not dollars.
